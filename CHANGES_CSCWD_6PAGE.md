## Storyline
Single thread throughout the paper: *temporal cues are extracted before aggregation; semantic adaptation is applied after the prediction pathways separate.* The method is presented as two designs:
- A. Pre-Aggregation Temporal Refinement (sparse kNN graph mix is described as a supporting refinement within A, not a separate contribution)
- B. Pathway-Restricted Semantic Adaptation

Title changed to "Pre-Aggregation Temporal Variation and Pathway-Restricted Semantic Adaptation for Weakly Supervised Video Anomaly Detection".
