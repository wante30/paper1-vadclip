\section{Sensitivity to the training protocol}
\label{app:protocol}

\input{tables/tab_protocol}

We repeated the XD-Violence comparison with VadCLIP's released training script, which evaluates on the test set after every epoch and keeps and reloads the best checkpoint. Under that script the retrained VadCLIP reaches \xdRelBase{} AP, \protoGapMean{} points above the test-independent baseline (\protoGapPos{} seeds). Within the released runs, the selected checkpoint exceeds the final-epoch checkpoint of the same run by \relLiftMean{} points, so checkpoint selection accounts for a small part of the difference between the protocols and the training trajectory for most of it. Under the released script, Stage~1 changes AP by \relStageOneMean{} points (\relStageOnePos{} seeds) and the full method by \relTMean{} points ($\pm$\relTSd, \relTPos{} seeds).

The released-script comparison covers XD-Violence, and the results in Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments} apply to training without test-set access. The part of the released procedure that produces the difference between protocols remains to be identified.
