\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We changed VadCLIP in two places, each targeting one design choice. Computing temporal variation before fixed-length pooling improves the classification output on UCF-Crime, while the same variation computed after pooling does not. Confining a semantic multiple-instance objective to the text-side adapter improves the semantic output on XD-Violence and leaves the classification output unchanged. Both gains are consistent across seeds under training without test-set access, and attribution experiments assign each to its stage. Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} examines how the comparison depends on the training protocol.
