\section{Conclusion}
We presented placement-aware refinement for CLIP-based weakly supervised video anomaly detection. Pre-aggregation variation refinement exposes raw snippet dynamics to the temporal model through aligned aggregation and a low-rank residual, complemented by a nearest-neighbor graph mixture. Pathway-restricted semantic adaptation adds anomaly supervision to an existing text-side adapter while preserving classification scores. Component, placement, and equal-budget ablations establish the roles of these designs. The complete method improves matched VadCLIP results on UCF-Crime and XD-Violence, reaches 84.94\% best-run AP under the original XD-Violence training setting, and adds less than 4\% neural scoring latency. These results support a lightweight extension that improves temporal evidence and semantic prediction through targeted computational placement.
