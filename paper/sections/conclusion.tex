\section{Conclusion}
We presented placement-aware refinement for CLIP-based weakly supervised video anomaly detection. An aligned low-rank residual supplies pre-aggregation variation, complemented by nearest-neighbor graph refinement. Pathway-restricted semantic adaptation adds anomaly supervision to an existing text-side adapter while preserving classification scores. Component, placement, and equal-budget ablations establish the roles of these designs. Under fixed-epoch evaluation, the complete method improves matched VadCLIP results by 0.41 AUC points on UCF-Crime and 1.66 AP points on XD-Violence. Under the original training settings, paired gains are $0.35\pm0.03$ AUC and $0.73\pm0.16$ AP points, respectively, with best-run results of 88.25\% AUC and 84.94\% AP. Neural scoring latency increases by less than 4\%. These results support a lightweight extension of VadCLIP through targeted computational placement.
