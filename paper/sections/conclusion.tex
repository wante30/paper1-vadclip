\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We changed VadCLIP in two places. Computing temporal variation before fixed-length pooling improves the classification output on UCF-Crime, and confining a semantic multiple-instance objective to the text-side adapter improves the semantic output on XD-Violence while leaving the classification output unchanged. Both gains hold on every seed when training does not access the test set. They do not hold on XD-Violence under the released training script, which selects checkpoints on test data; we have not identified the cause, and we did not run UCF-Crime under that protocol. Separating the effects of test-based selection and of the training trajectory is the next step.
