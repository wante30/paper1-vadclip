\caption{Overview of the proposed two-stage refinement. CLIP snippet features provide appearance and pre-aggregation variation cues. Stage~1 aggregates both streams with shared segment boundaries and injects variation through a zero-initialized residual, with sparse neighbourhood refinement in the temporal graph. VadCLIP then produces classification and semantic outputs from a shared representation. Stage~2 updates only the text-side $\mathrm{mlp}_1$ under the semantic MIL objective, leaving the classification pathway unchanged. Blue marks our refinement; grey denotes inherited or frozen VadCLIP computation.}
