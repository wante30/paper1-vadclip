\caption{Overview. Stage~1 computes snippet differences before fixed-length aggregation, pools them with the segment boundaries of the appearance stream, and adds them to $A$ through a zero-initialized residual. The similarity graph is mixed with a sparse $k$NN graph. Both outputs read the shared representation $h$, and $s^{(2)}$ depends on $s^{(1)}$ through the pooled vector $a$. Stage~2 trains $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CLASM}} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_G$ on $\mathrm{mlp}_1$ only, which lies downstream of $s^{(1)}$.}
